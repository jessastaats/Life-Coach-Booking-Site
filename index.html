COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you book.

Do I need to know exactly what I want to work on?+

No. “I know something needs to change, but I can’t name it yet” is a perfectly good place to begin.

Is coaching the same as therapy?+

No. Coaching is future-focused and goal-oriented. It does not diagnose or treat mental health conditions and is not a replacement for therapy or medical care.

What happens after I book?+

You’ll receive a confirmation with your appointment details, a short prep prompt, and your video-session link.

Can I integrate this with my real calendar?+

Yes. This front-end demo can be connected to Calendly, Acuity, Cal.com, Google Calendar, Microsoft Bookings, or a custom scheduling backend.
