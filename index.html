That is completely workable. “Something feels off and I can’t name it” is enough to start a coaching conversation.

No. Coaching is future-focused and goal-oriented. It does not diagnose or treat mental health conditions and is not a replacement for therapy or medical care.

Will I be pressured into a package afterward?+

No. A single session can stay a single session. If ongoing support makes sense, we can discuss it without a hard sell.
